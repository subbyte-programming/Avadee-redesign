COVERAGE

Insurance

Avadee Counseling lists Inland Empire Health Plan (IEHP) and Kaiser as accepted insurance plans. For other plans, a Superbill may be provided for you to submit to your carrier for possible reimbursement.

Coverage varies by plan. Contact your insurance provider to check mental-health benefits, deductibles, session limits, and whether written approval is required.

Questions to ask your insurer:

  • Does my plan include mental-health benefits?
  • Do I have a deductible, and have I met it?
  • Is there a limit on sessions per calendar year?
  • Is written approval from my primary-care physician required?

PAYMENT

Payment methods

The practice lists cash, debit cards, and major credit cards as payment methods. Ask the practice about any current payment questions before your appointment.

CANCELLATIONS

24-hour notice

If you cannot attend a session, the published policy asks that you cancel at least 24 hours beforehand. Otherwise, you may be charged the full session rate.

Fees and insurance details can change. Confirm the current information directly with Avadee Counseling and your insurance provider.
