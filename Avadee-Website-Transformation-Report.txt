WHAT WAS MISSING OR LESS DEVELOPED IN THE ORIGINAL (OBSERVED)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
These are design/experience differences seen in the pages reviewed, not
proof that the original website was broken or that every original page had
the same limitations:

- The main navigation was a text selector; it did not present the main
  destinations as the large, visual image-card menu created in the local
  redesign.
- The homepage used a conventional two-slide hero and separate content
  sections. It did not use the local version's scroll-controlled zoom and
  staged navigation reveal.
- The local version adds a visual gallery and image-led continuity between
  pages; this transition style was not observed on the original homepage and
  contact page reviewed.
- On the original contact page, the form displayed a detailed warning that
  messages could include health information sent through unencrypted email.
  Users should avoid sharing sensitive health information through any
  unprotected form or email channel.
- No claim is made here that the original website lacked mobile support,
  accessibility, search visibility, or working form delivery; those matters
  were not fully audited or tested for the original.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE CURRENT LOCAL REDESIGN ADDS OR CHANGES
-----------------------------------------------------------------
- Replaces the original WordPress page presentation with a static front-end
  composed of eight local HTML pages.
- Changes the hero and navigation to a calm, editorial style with image-card
  links and scroll-driven motion.
- Adds carousel controls/interactions to the service-detail pages and a
  scroll-to-next-page transition flow.
- Adds a floating Back to Top button across pages.
- Reorganizes selected practice, FAQ, rates, and contact information into
  dedicated local pages. Rates, insurance, contact details, and availability
  should be reconfirmed with the practice before launch because they may change.
- The local contact form is only a visual/validation preview. It does not
  deliver or store submissions; connect a secure form service if online
  inquiries are required.
- The local version currently has no dedicated local pages for the original
  Getting Started, Recent News, Mental Health Links, Physical Health Links,
  or Blog destinations. The original Privacy Policy link is also not in the
  current local main-page set. Confirm whether to restore/link these before
  replacing the live site.

0. WHAT THE ORIGINAL WEBSITE HAD (OBSERVED)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The original Avadee Counseling website was not an empty or non-working site.
It already had real practice information, service-page navigation, contact
details, appointment links, and a contact form. The following details were
observed on its live homepage and contact page:

Homepage:
- A top navigation selector with Home, About, Your Therapy, Getting Started,
  FAQs, Rates and Insurance, Appointment Request, Resources, Contact, and
  other entries.
- A header with the practice logo, phone number, email address, and a link
  to Therapy for Black Girls.
- A homepage slideshow with two numbered slide controls.
- A “Hope Starts Today” area with a “Start Here” appointment-request link.
- Practice introduction sections titled “In Practice,” “In Treatment,” and
  “My Commitment,” containing the therapist's approach and practice message.
- Shortcuts to About, Services, FAQs, and Contact.
- A footer linking to the Privacy Policy and displaying the WordPress,
  Genesis Framework, Bubbles theme, and Brighter Vision credits.

Contact page:
- The same navigation and contact header, including phone and email links.
- An introductory contact message and a “Start Here” appointment link.
- A “Send Me Your Questions” form with required name and email fields,
  optional phone and message fields, a required consent checkbox, and Submit.
- A visible notice explaining that the form could transmit health information
  through unencrypted email and describing the associated risks.
- A Privacy Policy link in the footer.

The original site therefore already provided useful information and ways to
contact the practice. This redesign changes the presentation and front-end
experience; it should not be described as adding those original contact
details, practice content, or appointment pathways from scratch.

Existing strengths of the original:
- Clear practice and therapist information, including the therapist's
  treatment approach and commitment.
- Direct phone and email links, appointment-request calls to action, and
  links to FAQs, rates, services, and resources.
- A contact form with consent text and a visible communication-risk notice.
- A Privacy Policy link.

Main opportunities identified in the pages reviewed:
- The navigation selector was functional but less visual and discoverable
  than a menu of clearly presented page cards.
- The homepage and Contact page used conventional layouts, rather than a
  consistent image-led journey with scroll-triggered transitions.
- The Contact page displayed a detailed risk notice for sending health
  information by unencrypted email; visitors should avoid submitting
  sensitive health details through an unprotected channel.
- These are design and experience observations, not evidence that the
  original site or its form was broken.

Items not carried into the current local page set / to confirm before launch:
- The original menu listed Getting Started and Resources, including Recent
  News, Mental Health Links, and Physical Health Links. These destinations
  are not among the eight main pages in the current local redesign.
- The original footer linked to a Privacy Policy; the current local page set
  should be checked with the client for whether and where that policy must be
  linked.
- The redesigned local contact form is only a preview and does not send
  messages. A secure form provider/backend must be connected if online
  submissions are required.

1. OVERVIEW
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Before:
- The original site was a WordPress-based counseling website with substantive
  practice copy, service navigation, a slideshow, and contact information.
- Its observed homepage used a conventional content-and-section layout; the
  observed contact page used a conventional text-and-form layout.
- The main navigation was presented as a selector rather than as a visual
  image-card exploration menu.
- The observed pages did not show the custom zoom-and-spin transitions used
  in this redesign.
- The original contact page did have a form and clearly disclosed the
  unencrypted-email health-information risk.

After:
- The local redesign presents the existing counseling brand and information
  in a warmer, more editorial visual style.
- It adds a scroll-driven hero zoom, image-card navigation, gallery movement,
  and image-led transitions between pages.
- It adds a floating back-to-top control and responsive layouts.
- It is a static front-end implementation; the redesigned local contact form
  is a preview and does not send or store messages.

Scope note: the specific “Before” observations above are based on the live
original homepage and contact page reviewed for this report. They are not a
claim that every original page or its mobile behavior was exhaustively audited.

10. KEY RESULTS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
- Added an editorial visual identity and a more immersive homepage.
- Added visual card navigation, gallery interactions, and page transitions.
- Added responsive styling and a floating back-to-top control.

  its local static front-end pages.
- The redesigned local form is not connected to message delivery; secure
  submission functionality remains a separate launch requirement.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
11. SUMMARY
-----------------------------------------------------------------
This redesign was not only about visual appearance. It was focused on making the website feel like a modern, high-end counseling brand with calm, premium motion, better content flow, and improved user experience across desktop and mobile.

The reviewed original site already contained substantial practice content,
contact methods, an appointment link, and a contact form. Its main observed
opportunity was a more visual, cohesive, and animated browsing experience.
The local redesign adds that presentation and interaction layer while keeping
the project static. It does not include a working message-submission backend.
The original Resources / Getting Started destinations and Privacy Policy
link should be confirmed with the client before the redesigned site is
published.
