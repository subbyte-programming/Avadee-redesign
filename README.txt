AVADEE COUNSELING — STATIC WEBSITE

Open index.html in a browser to preview the website. The site uses plain HTML,
CSS, and JavaScript and does not require a build step or backend.

The homepage is a focused introduction with an animated typographic marquee,
a softly rotating landscape image, and a scroll-driven image zoom that reveals
the navigation as the image fills the screen. About, Your Therapy, Depression
& Anxiety, Relationships, FAQs, and Rates & Insurance open as separate pages.
Counseling content, published rates, FAQ answers, and public contact details
are based on the Avadee Counseling website. Detail-page image carousels support
touch, drag, keyboard, trackpad, arrows, and dots.
The homepage navigation is an always-visible image-card section directly
beneath the scroll-zoom hero; its links open the counseling pages, FAQs, rates,
and contact. Inner pages retain the responsive full-screen card menu. On detail
pages, vertical or horizontal scrolling advances the image carousel; scrolling
past its final image spins that image into the next page. FAQ, rates, and contact
pages provide the same image-led continuation at the bottom of the page.
Every page also has a keyboard-accessible back-to-top control that appears
after scrolling and smoothly returns through the page's scroll animations.

The Connect navigation opens a separate full-screen contact page with a zoom
transition, telephone and email links, and the official appointment-request
link. Its form is a front-end preview only: it validates required fields and
explains that messages are not sent or stored. Before publishing, connect a
secure form service if form submissions are needed. Do not collect sensitive
health information through an unprotected form.

The page loads its typefaces from Google Fonts and its photography from
Unsplash, so an internet connection is needed for those assets. Replace the
image URLs in index.html with approved, locally hosted images before launch
if required.
